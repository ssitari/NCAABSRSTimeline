About the ratings

Team strength is measured using Sports Reference's Simple Rating System (SRS), which incorporates scoring margin and strength of schedule. Ratings are centered around each season's average, allowing long-term trajectories to be compared. Gold markers on a pinned or hovered line mark that team's NCAA tournament titles (hollow = later vacated).
